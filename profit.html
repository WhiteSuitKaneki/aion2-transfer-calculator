              How to flip without losing kinah
              • Beat the tax. The market keeps 10% of every sale, so you must resell for at least 11.1% more than you paid just to break even. "Break-even" on each row is that price.
              • Buy dips in things people always need. A sudden drop is usually one seller dumping a stack. Refining stones, Dragon Lord materials and potions come back; something a patch made useless won't.
              • Few sellers, set the price. With 8 or fewer listings you don't need to undercut. List at or a little above the cheapest.
              • Busy markets, undercut by 1. With lots of listings, only the cheapest sells first.
              • Sell into spikes. If something you hold jumped well above its weekly price, list it before sellers catch up.
              • Don't sink everything into one item. Spread kinah over several flips and buy only what you could resell within a few days.
              • Sales aren't reported for Siel. The listing count is the best hint of how busy an item is.
